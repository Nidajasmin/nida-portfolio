# Enrixa Store — Product Overview

**September 2026**

> Everything you get to run your online store

Enrixa Store helps any business open its own online store in minutes, with no technical skills needed.

Everything is in one place: your products, payments, delivery, design and sales reports. It is made for selling in India.

## 01 Getting started

### Open your store in minutes

- Sign up and get your own online store right away
- Add your staff and choose what each person can do
- Use your own website name (for example, yourshop.com)
- Start free, then upgrade to a paid plan when you grow
- Bring your products over from Shopify or WooCommerce

## 02 Products

### Show off what you sell

- Add products with photos, prices and descriptions
- Keep count of your stock and get warned when it runs low
- Offer choices like size and colour
- Sell extra warranty cover along with a product
- Group products into categories
- Let AI write product descriptions and create product photos

## 03 Selling & payments

### Easy buying for your customers

- Simple cart and checkout
- Send price quotes and turn them into orders
- Accept UPI, cards and net banking through Razorpay
- Send customers a payment link
- Offer cash on delivery
- Ship orders through Shiprocket
- Create discount coupons
- GST-ready bills and invoices
- Suggest related products to increase sales
- Customers can save favourites to a wishlist

## 04 Orders & customers

### Look after every buyer

- See all orders in one place and update their status
- See every customer, what they bought and how much they spent
- Cancelled orders put the stock back on the shelf
- Customers get their own account to track their orders
- Mark cash-on-delivery orders as paid
- Customers can reset a forgotten password themselves
- Unpaid orders are cleared away on their own
- Automatic order confirmation emails, in your own words

## 05 Your store’s pages

### Tell your story and build trust

- Add your own pages, such as About Us or Policies
- Customer reviews, which you approve before they appear
- Write blog posts
- Receive messages from a contact form
- Frequently asked questions page
- Collect newsletter sign-ups and send emails to them
- Show your shop locations on a store finder

## 06 Look & feel

### Make the store your own

- Change colours, fonts and layout, and see changes live
- Get design help from Genie, the built-in AI assistant
- Choose from ready-made designs
- Tell the Enrixa team about any design problem in one click

## 07 Reports & growth

### Know what is working

- Sales dashboard showing what needs your attention
- Understand where shoppers stop before buying
- See how many people visit your store
- Helps your store show up on Google
- See which products and pages are most popular
- Works with Google Analytics
- See how many people are shopping right now
- WhatsApp chat button for customers

## 08 Custom websites

### For businesses that want a one-of-a-kind site

- Have a fully custom website built that still runs on Enrixa
- Swap website pictures yourself, without calling a developer
- Your web team can publish updates and undo them in one click
- Connect Enrixa to your other business tools

## 09 Safe & reliable

### Peace of mind, built in

- Your store’s information stays private
- Alerts when something needs you
- Totals are always exact, to the paisa
- Enrixa team keeps every store running
